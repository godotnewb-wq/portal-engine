Before the Burn, Faeworld was home to many peoples. Kingdoms rose and fell, sorcerers studied the Aether Arts, and knights hunted dragons. About five hundred years ago scholars found the Lattice in the Aether, and soon the off-worlders’ ships came; after a short period of tension, Faeworld joined galactic society.
