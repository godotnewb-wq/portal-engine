The lower stairs switch back from the pumphouse down to the water, lined with workshops, noodle counters and lift shafts; Brask’s workshop is among them. The Drowned Saints take their tithe at the landing halfway down. Votive tins are nailed to the rail, one lift only goes down, and water runs under the steps.
