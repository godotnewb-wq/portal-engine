Pump Station Nine is a pre-Burn pump station under the Ninth Turn. Its upper galleries are dry; its lower level fills with the tide around midnight, and raw Aether glows in the water. Wheel-valves stand taller than a person, green light moves on the ceiling, and old Council stencils show under the rust. The Drowned Saints skim Aether from it, and Grid surveyors have mapped it.
