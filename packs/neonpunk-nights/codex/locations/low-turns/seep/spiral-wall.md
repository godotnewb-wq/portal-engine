The Spiral Wall is a Garden Guard shrine where raw Aether weeps down a cracked wall in a slow spiral. Keepers tend it; people bring offerings of bread and cheap wire, and golems bring their rune-cores to be blessed. Wet chalk spirals cover it, and the stone hums.
