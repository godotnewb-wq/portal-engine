Kettle Court is a courtyard of shared housing around a refugee network’s kitchen, where anyone can eat once a day. The job board is a wall of chalk and pinned paper in a dozen languages. The kettle never comes off the fire, children run messages, and a Korrath elder knows everyone’s business.
