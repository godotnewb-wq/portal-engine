The Seep is the drainage district below the Ninth Turn, where raw Aether seeps through the walls and pools in the gutters: damp, crowded and warm. Most of the city’s refugees live here, beside deep elf families who were here first. The refugee networks hold sway, and the Garden Guard at its shrine.
