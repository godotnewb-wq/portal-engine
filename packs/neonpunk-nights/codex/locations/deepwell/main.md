The Deepwell is the chasm at the city’s base where the Aether wells up from the planet’s core: half-ruined, raw, and strong enough to sicken almost anyone. The Garden Guard tends its edge. Almost nobody else goes down, and the ones who do come back Aether-sick or don’t come back. Monsters and wild magic live there.
