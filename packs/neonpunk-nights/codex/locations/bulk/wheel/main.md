The Wheel is the market district, built in and around a vast pre-Burn turntable that no longer turns, with stalls, workshops, clinics and flats stacked around its rim. Loud, cheap and busy at every hour. Traders, artisans, Couriers, nurses and apprentices work here. The Couriers, the market traders and the Wardens, who actually patrol here, hold sway.
