The Kiln substation feeds the Kilnworks and the Wheel; when the Bulk goes dark, it goes dark from here. Meter-men and line crews in green-banded coats come and go, a deep hum runs through the street, and a Garden Guard spiral keeps being scrubbed off its gate. A schedule is pinned inside the guard hut, face to the wall.
