The Kilnworks is the foundry district under the Coil Line’s mid-span: chimneys, heat, shift whistles, and furnace glow through every window at night. Foundry hands, Grid line crews, Coil Line staff and shift workers going home fill it. The Grid holds sway through the substation, the Council through the line, and the foundries through everyone’s wages.
