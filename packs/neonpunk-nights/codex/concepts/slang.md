Slang in the Low Turns and the Bulk: a lantern-pour is the cheapest drink; grid-sick is dizzy or nauseous from raw Aether; drop a cinder means pay up; a dead span is rail with no grid power; coil-sick is dizzy from a drive coil’s field; riding the hatch is climbing onto a tram’s roof; meter-men are Grid staff who read and cut meters; the tithe is what the Drowned Saints take on the stairs.
