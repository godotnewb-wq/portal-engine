Below the Bulk, cooled pipes sweat along every ceiling. Conduits glow with leaked Aether, green-gold when it’s raw and cold white when it’s refined. Rune-lamps, drive coils, cinder readers and Grid meters hum in every room, and when the grid surges every light in a building flickers at once.
