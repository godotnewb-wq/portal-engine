The Aether is Faeworld’s magic, unique to it, as many worlds have their own. It rises from the planet’s core and wells up strongest in the chasm at the city’s base. Magic and tech grew as one craft, so all gear is magitech. Native magic weakens far from its world, so off-world magic is almost never seen here; refugees lost their homeworld’s magic with their homes.
