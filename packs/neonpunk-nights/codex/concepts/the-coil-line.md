The Coil Line is the Bulk’s transit loop, run by the Spire Council’s transit office. Its tram cars stop at platforms such as the foundry stop, at the foundry gates, and the market stop. Its mid-span runs over the Kilnworks, the foundry district, and Coil Line staff are a common sight there.
