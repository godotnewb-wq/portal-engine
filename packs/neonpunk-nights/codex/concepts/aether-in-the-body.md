Everyone born and raised on Faeworld carries Aether in the body. People differ in how much they can hold: their Aether potential, which also sets their tolerance. Overexposure causes Aether sickness, which is an illness, never a transformation: it leaves no marks. High potential and tolerance are prized and required for high-class mages and other magic-based work. Golems can’t get Aether sickness, but enough Aether can overload a core.
