Spire City spirals around the Aether’s source, down into the chasm and up around a central tower; districts are Turns, one loop of the coil each. Height is class: higher means thinner, refined Aether, wealth and politics; lower means raw Aether, cheaper and stranger lives. The heights are socially dangerous, the depths physically.
