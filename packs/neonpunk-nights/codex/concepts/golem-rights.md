Golem personhood came through generational change over a long time, not one movement or victory, so the cause has no name and no organization. A loose spread of lawyers, activists, community groups and a shrinking hardline minority carry on the leftover fights over old ownership contracts and corporate claims to rune-core designs. Some think the work is nearly done; others think it barely started. Never give the cause a name.
