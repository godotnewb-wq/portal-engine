Faeworld is part of galactic society but widely considered a backwater. Space travel is for the rich; the spaceport in the Crown is the only door off-world. Off-worlders, aliens, are found in every class, from aristocratic Houses to refugees. Galactic events reach the city only as news, rumor, prices and the occasional off-world artifact.
