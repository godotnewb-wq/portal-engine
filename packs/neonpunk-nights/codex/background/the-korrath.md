The Korrath are the largest refugee people in Spire City. The Stillness took their homeworld, and more of the city’s refugees fled it than any other people. In the Low Turns they are part of the life around the refugee networks’ kitchens and shared housing, the largest of which is at Kettle Court in the Seep.
