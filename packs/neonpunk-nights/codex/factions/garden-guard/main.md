The Garden Guard is the Animist order that tends the Aether’s source and the shrines where it rises, and calls the grid a theft. Keepers tend small spiral shrines where raw Aether seeps through walls, bless rune-cores for golems who ask, and protest at Grid sites. Their sign is a chalked spiral; it turns up on doors near Grid work, and people disagree about whether it’s a blessing or a warning. Most keepers are gentle; some are not. Usual titles: keeper, gardener, elder.
