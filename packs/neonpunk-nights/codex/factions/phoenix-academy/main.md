The Phoenix Academy is the city’s great school of the Aether, in the Crown, trying to recover what was lost in the Burn. It takes a handful of scholarship students a year by entrance exam each spring; most hopefuls study from borrowed books. Its scholars argue Mechanist against Animist against True Source in public lectures half the city quotes and nobody attends. Usual titles: student, scholar, fellow, professor, dean.
