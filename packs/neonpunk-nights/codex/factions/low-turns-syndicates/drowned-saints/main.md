The Drowned Saints are the syndicate of the Ninth Turn. They hold the lower stairs and the flooded pump stations under them, skim raw Aether from the water, and take a “tithe” from everyone who uses the stairs. They dress the part: votive tin, drowned-saint tattoos, a habit of blessing people they’ve just robbed. Their boss is called the Abbess. Usual titles: saint (a member), deacon (trusted), the Abbess (leadership).
