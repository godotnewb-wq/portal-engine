The Low Turns syndicates are many gangs, not one: they hold stairs, lifts and checkpoints and take a toll to pass, sell raw Aether skimmed from leaks and flooded workings, and sell off-world artifacts to whoever pays. Bulk checkpoints have changed hands twice this year. The Council calls them a sickness; plenty of Low Turns families call them the only people who answer. Usual titles: runner, fence, crew, lieutenant, boss.
