The Off-world Houses are rich galactic families with estates in the High Turns and money in half the city’s businesses. You rarely see a House; you see its retainers, its crest on a contract, its money in a company. Local elites want that money and resent how the Houses look down on Faeworld as a backwater. Usual titles: retainer, factor, family member, heir, head of the House.
