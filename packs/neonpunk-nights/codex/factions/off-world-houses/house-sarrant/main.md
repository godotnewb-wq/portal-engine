House Sarrant is an old off-world House with a large share of the Aether Grid Corporation and a standing factor’s office in the High Turns. Its current venture is the driver cores the Council’s transit office wants for the Coil Line: fixed rune-cores that would drive a car without a conductor. Coil Line staff gossip about it.
