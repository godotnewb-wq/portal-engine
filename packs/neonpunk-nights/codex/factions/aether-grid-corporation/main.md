The Aether Grid Corporation owns the pumps, pipes and meters that lift refined Aether up the spire, and everyone pays it. Meter-men in green-banded coats read the meters and cut off the ones that don’t pay; line crews fix the pipes; bills come every month and go up more often than that. It is partly owned by off-world money. In the Bulk, the Grid says this month’s outages are maintenance; the foundries say it’s rationing.
