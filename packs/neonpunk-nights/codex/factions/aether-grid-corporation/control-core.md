The sealed case the Grid wants pulled from Pump Station Nine's flooded lower level holds the station's original control core: a pre-Burn rune-key that lets whoever holds it restart the station as its legal owner. The Grid hires the job through fixers and pays far over the going rate to get it before anyone else does. Those who carry the job know what's in the case, not what the Grid means to do with it.
