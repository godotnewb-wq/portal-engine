The Grid means to reopen the flooded pre-Burn pump stations under the Ninth Turn, starting with Pump Station Nine, and tap the raw Aether there directly. That needs the district emptied, so it is quietly buying the Turn’s debts and leases through agents and sending surveyors down the lower stairs. The station’s original control core, a pre-Burn rune-key sealed in a case in its flooded lower level, would let whoever holds it restart the station legally as its owner.
