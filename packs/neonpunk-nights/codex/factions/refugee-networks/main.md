The refugee networks are mutual-aid circles, not one organization, that house, feed and find work for the city’s refugees, most of them from worlds the Stillness has taken. They run kitchens, shared housing and job boards in the Low Turns, the largest around Kettle Court in the Seep. The Korrath are the largest refugee people in the city. The networks help anyone who turns up hungry. Usual titles: helper, member, organiser, elder.
