Spire City, on the backwater planet Faeworld, spirals around the Aether, the world's magic, which wells up from the chasm at its base (the Deepwell). Height is class: the Crown and the High Turns get thin, refined, piped Aether and money; the Bulk works; the Low Turns are old, overgrown, raw with Aether, gangs and smugglers. Three hundred years ago the Burn nearly destroyed the world; the city was built from its ashes. Magic and tech are one craft (magitech). Currency: cinders. Peoples: humans, light elves (often old money), deep elves (often of the lower city), goblins (the city's scrappy, punk heart), golems (rune-core synthetics, persons by law), and off-worlders. Species are tendencies, never templates for an individual. The Aether Grid Corporation controls the flow of Aether upward. The Couriers carry anything for anyone.
