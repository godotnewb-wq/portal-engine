# app

The Electron app. It shows state and forwards input; it holds no game logic.

- `main/`: creates the window; owns the chosen pack, its save store and its play logs (`PlayLogs`); IPC handlers for packs, settings, turns, scene ends, saves, log sessions, flags and turn details. **The bookkeeper** (milestone 4c): when a turn is returned, the main process starts its bookkeeper call and holds it by the turn's id; the next turn, either half of the scene's end and a save wait for it (up to the answering profile's timeout), apply its records to the state they were sent, and send `catching-up` / `caught-up` scene events while they wait, which the scene screen shows as "Catching up…". The call doesn't hold the one-turn lock; a state whose last turn isn't the held one ignores it.
- `preload/`: exposes the IPC as `window.api` (the contract is `ipc.ts`).
- `renderer/`: React screens. The pack picker comes first, then the pack's title screen, the scene, the summary, saves and settings. Reading logic is in `ui/reading.ts`.

Logs (milestone 2): a log session begins when a game is started or loaded (`beginLog`) and ends at the title screen or on quitting. On the scene screen, **Details** opens the Turn details overlay for the turn on screen (milestone 4d; `ui/TurnDetails.tsx` in the shared full-screen `ui/DetailsOverlay.tsx`; `Esc` or Close shuts it, and the reading keys wait while it is open). Its tabs are grouped by meaning: **Turn** (the input, the plan's moves and change, timings, beats with flags and AI scores; a beat jumps the reader to it), **People** (each character present: the brief's always-on tokens, details chosen and cut, memories recalled, feeling and what was shown, reads), **Secrets and knowledge** (the secret display, what came out, leaks, guesses and claims), **Lore** (entries by layer with rough tokens, the lore lines), **Checks** (breakdowns, honored or not by the bookkeeper), **Fixes** (code findings, corrections, the rewrite), **Calls** (each model call; clicking one fetches its prompt and reply, `callText`) and **Records** (the turn's bookkeeping record, or "Catching up…" until it answers, refetched on open). A tab whose part the log doesn't hold says "not recorded". On the summary screen, **Details** opens the scene-end overlay (`ui/SceneEndDetails.tsx`): **People** (each witness's meter changes with before and after, and the reason; a failed call says so), **Ties** (stated, applied, asked of the player), **Memory** (each memory and its level) and **Calls**; `sceneTies` and `sceneEnd` return it as `details`. Both overlays build their tabs with `turnTabs` and `sceneEndTabs`, which `tests/app/details.test.ts` draws one by one from a scene played, logged and read back (milestone 4d task 3). The text box shows "turn N · beat n of m". **F** (or the ⚑ button) opens the flag panel on the beat being read, **Shift+F** on the whole turn: `1`/`2`/`3` rate, reasons are tags, typing fills the note, `Enter` saves, `Esc` cancels. F does nothing while focus is in a text field, so it never types into the player's line. Only logged turns can be flagged; flags go to the current session's `flags.jsonl` and name the session that recorded the turn. Settings has the logging switch and **Open logs folder**.

The renderer runs in a browser context, so it imports platform code only as types (checked in `tests/architecture.test.ts`).
