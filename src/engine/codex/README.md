# engine/codex

The codex: a pack's lore as one Markdown file per entry under `codex/`, read into entries the prompt layers will draw on (milestone 3). Each file has a small header in a YAML subset (`header.ts`, parsed by hand so the engine imports only zod) and a body that is the entry's full text. The id comes from the path: the top folder names the kind (`locations`, `factions`, `species`, `philosophies`, `concepts`, `events`, `world`), each folder below adds a part, and `main.md` takes its folder's id.

- **Locations are the map tree:** `locations/<tier>/<region>/<place>.md`, so a place's id carries its region and tier (`locationChain`). Layer 1 must be a setting tier; nothing goes deeper than layer 3; every layer above an entry must have its own entry.
- **Factions** live in a folder per faction; sub-groups are subfolders following the setting's `parent` links. `main.md` is the faction's own entry; other files beside it are deeper entries belonging to it.
- **Knowledge:** `common`, `circles` (who's inside: `circle`, defaulting to the entry's faction at member and above, plus `also`) or `secret` (`known-by`, optional `cap`). Selectors: `<faction> [level+]`, `occupation <words>`, `tier <id>`, `species <id>`, `character <id>`, on the engine's four faction levels (`@engine/world`).
- **Checks:** `buildCodex` reports unreadable files and headers (file and line); `codexProblems` reports what spans the pack (duplicate ids and keywords, alias clashes, text over the ruleset's `codex.fullWords`, a summary over `LORE_LINE_WORDS` (it stands in for a lore line that runs long), secrets open to the writer, misplaced stage lines and selectors, unknown factions, tiers, species, characters and related ids, the map tree, location files' `parent`); `codexWarnings` flags the pack's stance words in codex text.

**Layers** (`layers.ts`, claude/lore-injection.md §5): `directorStage` (the primer plus every common entry's `stage` line, in a fixed order: world, places, peoples, worldviews, factions, concepts), `writerStage` (the tone plus `namesLine`: the names of common places, factions, worldviews and world background, with aliases), and `loreLayers` for one turn: the **scene-scoped** layer (the place's chain from its codex id, which is its location file's `parent` plus its id; the factions, walked up through sub-groups, and peoples' culture of those present; linked entries as summaries) and the **triggered** layer (entries whose keywords, name or aliases appear in what the player said, any case, or in what the model wrote, where a one-word name or alias the codex capitalizes counts only when capitalized; simple plurals, a leading "the" ignored and words inside character names skipped; a faction named brings its other entries). **Insider entries** come in only when someone present knows them, marked with who (`knowledge.ts`: `knows`, `fits`, `standings`, `mayHold`; a faction level counts one step lower in each parent, never above trusted; occupation, species and named-character selectors; a tier selector fits no one until characters record where they live); an insider entry someone's occupation opens comes with them in the scene layer. Secrets never come through the layers. `stanceLines` gives the official stances among the factions in play, one way, in the pack's words; a stance toward a faction covers its sub-groups unless they have their own. Each layer keeps to its budget (the ruleset's `codex.sceneTokens`, default 1200, and `codex.triggeredTokens`, default 800) by dropping the least specific entries to summaries, then out. An entry linked as a summary in the scene layer may also come in full in the triggered layer when it comes up, so the scene layer (in the cached part of the prompt) doesn't change turn to turn. Same inputs, same text.

**Names and secrets** (`names.ts`, claude/lore-injection.md §11): `nameIndex` holds the canon names (place, world, worldview and concept entries, faction main entries, aliases, capitalized keywords, the setting's names), the pack's generic words and the people, who are never checked; `withProseNames` adds the new-looking names the pack's own prose uses. `checkNames` finds capitalized runs in prose (joined by "of" or "of the"; a possessive ends one) and reports a `wrong-name` when a run shares a distinctive word with a canon name or is a typo away from one (one letter for short words, two from seven letters), naming the right one, and an `invented-name` when a run pairs a generic word with a new one ("the Ada Gang"). Two canon names side by side pass, and a sentence's opening word isn't part of the name. `secretLeaks` finds a codex secret in prose by its name, a multi-word keyword, or `LEAK_WORDS` words in a row from its text, except secrets someone in the scene is keeping.

**Public API** (`@engine/codex`): `parseEntryFile`, `buildCodex`, `idFromPath`, `entryById`, `locationChain`, `codexProblems`, `codexWarnings`, `LORE_LINE_WORDS`, `nameIndex`, `withProseNames`, `candidates`, `checkName`, `checkNames`, `secretLeaks`, `LEAK_WORDS`, `parseSelector`, `selectorText`, `directorStage`, `writerStage`, `namesLine`, `canonNames`, `loreLayers`, `triggeredEntries`, `codexFactionId`, `tokensOf`, `stanceLines`, `knows`, `fits`, `standings`, `mayHold`, `Knower`, `CodexEntry`, `CodexFile`, `Selector`, `CODEX_KINDS`, `CODEX_FOLDERS`, `KNOWLEDGE`, `AUDIENCES`.
