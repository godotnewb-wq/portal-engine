# engine/relationships

The end of a scene, in two calls per witness (present or departed), outside the turn budget (milestone 4c; decided 2026-11-16, user). First the **relationship call** (`relationship.ts`, `judgeScene`), all witnesses at once: each judges, as themselves, how each relationship meter changed toward the player and toward the other witnesses, in the ruleset's scale words (the ruleset maps the words to numbers), the feelings the scene left them with, and what someone here became to them or stopped being. The game settles those ties (`ties.ts`), the player answers any that wait on them, and then the **memory call** (`endScene`), all witnesses at once, writes what each remembers, told what changed and shown that witness's own relationship answers (kept on the scene as `scene.judged`), so the two can't disagree. Everything is applied together when the scene closes (`applySummaries`): memories are stored, meters move (kept within the ruleset's range), and relations are created where witnesses had none, starting at the ruleset's `relations.start` (a stranger: neutral). **What the player found out** is the game's record, not a witness's say: each of a witness's own secrets given away on screen this scene (`scene.told`) becomes a fact the player knows. The relationship call goes through what each person here already is to the witness (did this scene make, end or change it: vows make a spouse, a breakup said and meant makes an ex), and one job hired for or offered, an order given once or one favor is no tie (milestone 4c, after its first scored run). Both calls are told what was claimed in front of the witness and never confirmed (milestone 4c: a leak the bookkeeper recorded, as the speaker's claim), and the memory call keeps a guess, a suspicion or an unconfirmed claim uncertain in the memory, never a fact learned; a successful read of someone keeping a secret tells the reader's memory call that something was hidden, never what. A failed relationship call changes no meters, feelings or ties for that witness; a failed memory call writes a plain memory; either is reported with the witness.

**Aftermath** (`aftermath.ts`, milestone 4b; npc design §7): the relationship call also returns how the witness feels as the scene ends (`feels`), feelings toward one person it left them with (`toward`: who, a shade, intensity and why) and who a feeling they carry was settled with (`resolved`). `applyCarried` checks each against the ruleset's shades (never a mood-only family) and keeps them in `state.aftermath`: a feeling toward someone, renewed when the same family is already active toward them, the weakest replaced past `aftermath.towardMax`; general aftermath from `feels`, or from their mood as the scene ends when the call gives none, when it is at least `aftermath.minIntensity` or the scene gave them a Core memory, the weakest replaced past `aftermath.max`. Resolved, replaced and faded feelings toward someone go into `state.endedFeelings`; a new one in a family that ended toward the same person within `aftermath.historyDays` is a **repeat**, and the game adds the ruleset's growing change (`aftermath.repeat`) to the meter `aftermath.meters` maps the family to. Tags never change here. Everyone who was there has now seen the player (`state.lastSeen`). A tie change in the scene (`scene.tieEvents`, relationships §3) makes the memory of both sides Core, whatever the call chose, and the summary is told what changed.

**Memories** (milestone 4b; memory §3–4): the memory call returns several memories, most important first (`memories`: text, level, why), and the ids of earlier ones the scene revisited (`revisited`); it is shown the witness's recalled memories, and any the director's brief brought in during the scene, with ids. The game keeps at most the tier's `memory.perScene`; a game-forced event (a tie change they are part of, or one of their secrets given away on screen) makes the first one Core outside the limits; other Core needs a why, fits the tier's `memory.core` limit and passes the over-grading guard (something felt in the scene reached `memory.guard`), or it is Normal. Revisited memories go up a level (Normal to Core only with a forced event or room under the limit), restart their fading clock and come back into recall. The report lists each memory, the revisits and what the game changed (`memoryNotes`). The summary format from before 4b (one `memory` and `level`) is still read. **Learned secrets** (5b): the memory call is shown other people's secrets that came out in front of the witness this scene (`Turn.exposed`) and returns a sentence of memory for each (`heard`); everyone it came out in front of learns it, with the game's own sentence for one the call leaves out, once, as a Normal memory outside the limits carrying the secret's fact id and, for a linked secret, its codex pointer (`Memory.secret`, `Memory.codex`). Both calls are shown what the witness carries, the ended feelings toward the people there and how long since a close tie saw the player; the relationship call also gets the shade list, what each person here is to them now and the words a tie can be. `fade` and `dailyUpkeep` lose a point of intensity every `aftermath.fadeDays` in-game days, worked out from the days passed, so running them twice changes nothing; `nextDay` moves the clock to the next morning (the ruleset's `clock.morning`) between scenes and runs the upkeep. The prompt shows each meter as its word band, never a number; there is no derived relation label (milestone 4a: relationship tags replace it), only an authored relation's `note`. A failed call costs only that witness.

**Relationship changes** (`ties.ts`, milestone 4c; relationships §3; decided 2026-11-16, replacing 4b's tie check, which read the pair from outside and got the direction wrong): each witness's relationship call states ties in the first person, `{"with", "now" | "no_longer", "lasting"?, "line"}` ("Ada is now my wife"), using a tag's id, one of its words (kept as that side's word) or the word their own tie already uses. `settleTies` settles them across the witnesses: a tag that isn't one-sided **begins** only when both people's calls say it, a tag on one side and its counterpart on the other, and one side alone or two sides that differ change nothing (`alone`); a tag **ends** on one call; a one-sided tag, and a tie with the player, need one call; a statement that both ends and begins a tag carries the ending with the beginning, so it never applies alone. The quoted line must be in the scene (`findLine`: forgiving about punctuation, case, a speaker's name in front and a few words off; it reads the player's words too), or the statement is refused. A new tag the ruleset's `ties.lasting` names (Milestone 5 plan, task 5: work ties) begins only when the call's `lasting` word is one the ruleset marks lasting (say, *steady* rather than *one job*); without one it is refused, so a hire for one job makes no client. The call is shown each tag's `means` beside its words ("client (who hires Ada)"), so a work tie runs the right way. Each change still passes `tieProblem`; an arrangement with the player waits in `scene.pendingTies` for their yes (`answerTie`), asked before the memory calls. In a group the ruleset marks `oneAtATime`, a tag that begins ends the one held; a tag that ends on its own becomes its `endsAs` tag. A change keeps its line (`TieEvent.line`) and leaves the relationship's note as it was, since the first person gives no reason. Tie events force a Core memory for both, as before. Logged as a `ties` record in the scenes file (each witness's answers, what was stated, applied, pending, refused and alone); the scene-end trace keeps the player's answers.

**Topics and consolidation** (5c; memory §7): each turn records the codex entries its conversation brought up and the secrets that came out (`Turn.topics`); the summary is shown them and tags each memory with ids from that list only (`Memory.topics`; others are dropped and noted). Each memory also records who it is actually about (`Memory.focus`, kept apart from who was there, `about`): the people the summary names, plus anyone the scene changed something with (a tie change, a feeling toward them, meters toward the player that moved, the player finding out one of their secrets or reading them). A memory's subjects are its topics and the people it is about, the player only when it is about them; never who was merely there, and never the place (`subjectsOf`; 5c follow-up). `consolidationGroups` finds, per character, at least `memory.consolidate.min` memories on one subject that may merge (Normal or Fleeting, in recall, older than `memory.consolidate.days`, never merged before), each memory in one group, the most specific subject first (topic, person, then the player). `consolidate` runs one background call per group (`buildConsolidationPrompt`, role `consolidation`); `applyConsolidation` makes a Normal memory of at most `memory.consolidate.words` words with the originals' people (who was there and who it is about), place (when shared), topics and learned-secret pointers (`Memory.from`, `Memory.inherited`), and takes the originals out of recall, linked (`Memory.mergedInto`). A failed call or a refused text leaves the originals as they were. The app runs it after Next day's upkeep (IPC `consolidate`, the summary model), logged in the scenes file.

**Public API** (`@engine/relationships`): `consolidate`, `consolidationGroups`, `buildConsolidationPrompt`, `parseConsolidation`, `applyConsolidation`, `subjectsOf`, `subjectWords`, `ConsolidationTrace`, `sceneTopics`, `nextDay`, `dailyUpkeep`, `fade`, `applyCarried`, `CarriedReport`, `judgeScene(pack, state, summarize, now?)` (the relationship calls and the ties settled: the state with `scene.judged`, tie events and pending ties, and a `RelationshipTrace`), `endScene(pack, state, summarize, now?)` (the memory calls and the close: the new state, the reports and a `SceneEndTrace`), `buildRelationshipPrompt`, `parseRelationship`, `settleTies`, `findLine`, `buildMemoryPrompt`, `parseSummary`, `applySummaries`, `plainSummary`, `addMeters`, `witnesses`, `transcript`, `WitnessReport`.
