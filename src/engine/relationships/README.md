# engine/relationships

The end of a scene: one summary call per witness (present or departed), all at once and outside the turn budget. Each witness judges, as themselves, how each relationship meter changed toward the player and toward the other witnesses, in the ruleset's scale words; the ruleset maps the words to numbers. Memories are stored, meters move (kept within the ruleset's range), secrets that truly came out become facts the player knows, and relations are created where witnesses had none, starting at the ruleset's `relations.start` (a stranger: neutral).

**Aftermath** (`aftermath.ts`, milestone 4b; npc design §7): each summary also returns how the witness feels as the scene ends (`feels`), feelings toward one person it left them with (`toward`: who, a shade, intensity and why) and who a feeling they carry was settled with (`resolved`). `applyCarried` checks each against the ruleset's shades (never a mood-only family) and keeps them in `state.aftermath`: a feeling toward someone, renewed when the same family is already active toward them, the weakest replaced past `aftermath.towardMax`; general aftermath from `feels`, or from their mood as the scene ends when the summary gives none, when it is at least `aftermath.minIntensity` or the scene gave them a Core memory, the weakest replaced past `aftermath.max`. Resolved, replaced and faded feelings toward someone go into `state.endedFeelings`; a new one in a family that ended toward the same person within `aftermath.historyDays` is a **repeat**, and the game adds the ruleset's growing change (`aftermath.repeat`) to the meter `aftermath.meters` maps the family to. Tags never change here. Everyone who was there has now seen the player (`state.lastSeen`). The summary is shown what the witness carries, the ended feelings toward the people there, how long since a close tie saw the player, and the shade list. `fade` and `dailyUpkeep` lose a point of intensity every `aftermath.fadeDays` in-game days, worked out from the days passed, so running them twice changes nothing; `nextDay` moves the clock to the next morning (the ruleset's `clock.morning`) between scenes and runs the upkeep. The prompt shows each meter as its word band, never a number; there is no derived relation label (milestone 4a: relationship tags replace it), only an authored relation's `note`. A failed call costs only that witness.

**Public API** (`@engine/relationships`): `nextDay`, `dailyUpkeep`, `fade`, `applyCarried`, `CarriedReport`, `endScene(pack, state, summarize, now?)` (returns the new state, the reports and a `SceneEndTrace` for the logs), `buildSummaryPrompt`, `parseSummary`, `applySummaries`, `plainSummary`, `addMeters`, `witnesses`, `transcript`, `WitnessReport`.

**Depends on:** core, rules, characters, pack, state, memory.
**Design:** `relationships.md` §1. **Tuning:** the ruleset's `meters`, `wordScale` and `relations`.
