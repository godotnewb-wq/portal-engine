# engine/characters

What a character file holds: tier (recurring or major: how detailed a character is; being in the party is a separate list in the game state), species, look, role, faction (with an optional `level` on the engine's four levels, a free-text `rank` title and an `occupation`; any may be blank), personality, portrait (how they are with strangers and people they know, what they notice, habits: the writer's picture of them), agenda, contradictions, voice card (rhythm, vocabulary, slang, what they avoid, sample lines, and how they sound angry, warm and lying), mood, knowledge, values, secrets with tells (personal, in free text, or linked: a `codex` pointer to a codex secret with the holder's `role` and `angle`, whose text is filled in when the pack loads; a personal secret may add a short `summary` and `keywords` for the director's sheet), starting meters toward the player (signed, around the ruleset's neutral point), relations to others in the cast (relationship `tags` from the ruleset, each with an optional display `word` and `private` flag; meters; and a one-line `note` of history between them), an optional `playerTie` (what the player is to them: tags and a note), and a stat block (required for anyone in the starting party). The full card (milestone 4a; npc design §3–8) adds, in the ruleset's words: an `age` stage, `haunts` (codex locations with times and days), `tells`, an afraid voice shift, `layers` (personality axes, attachment, values with strength and optional personal wording, foundation sensitivities; never shown to the writer as labels), `expression` (masking and display lines per emotion family, optional shade lines), `gates` (per-action base and fixed words) and `blockers`, `motives`, `malleability` and `fieldLocks`, and per-secret `disclose` minimums. Also the player.

**Public API** (`@engine/characters`): `CharacterSchema`, `PlayerSchema`, `VoiceCardSchema`, `SecretSchema`, `MetersSchema` (meters by the ruleset's ids), gate profiles (`gates.ts`: `requirement` and `requirementRung` work a requirement out from the base word, link shifts, the largest motive shift down to its floor, fixed words and blockers; `gateMet` for an action; `wordsMet` for authored disclosure minimums), and their types.

Species, factions, skills and meters are the pack's; `engine/pack` checks a character against them.

**Depends on:** core, rules.
**Design:** `director writer.md` §7, `npc design.md` §2.
